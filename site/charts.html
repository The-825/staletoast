    How to read the chart.

    Charts retain dated editions, prior positions, peaks and appearances. First appearances show NEW; earlier movement is never guessed. The review priority shows which sources we plan to investigate first, not which are best. Entries with the same number are tied, and their display order is not a quality difference. Edition dates mark an editorial reevaluation of recorded evidence, not a fresh check of every source. The full source ledger stays uncapped when a source falls off the chart.
