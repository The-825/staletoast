Collaborative intelligence

What studies found. How others built it.

StaleToast is a public library of research and repositories about people and AI systems working together. Each record shows what was read, how closely, and where the original source lives, so you can judge what to trust.

Neither research nor repositories carries a quality certification. A record means it was screened or reviewed to the depth shown, nothing more.
