What do the directness levels mean?
  • Target-like (D3): studied work close to the setting this guide cares about.
  • Adjacent (D2): studied a nearby setting, so findings may carry over in part.
  • Analogy (D1): studied a comparable but different setting. Use it as an analogy.
  • Conceptual (D0): a concept or argument, not a study of this kind of work.

The time span runs from not observed to observed over a long period. Recorded order sorts directness first and time span second. It is attention order, not a quality verdict.
