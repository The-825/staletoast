Screening and promotion

Automated intake vs. human source screening.

Source-screened records

Papers and repositories that received a structured human or bounded methods screen carry recorded evidence depth, linked topics, and explicit transfer limits. Only source-screened records are eligible for the Hot 100.

Promoted catalog papers are distinguished from unpromoted candidates so early method screens never masquerade as settled findings.

Automated relevance checks

Abstract and README relevance checks expand discovery breadth while awaiting human appraisal. They carry no directness or horizon grade, leave all repository mechanisms at Unknown, and sort after appraised records.

Held sources in the public dispositions register stay retained for provenance and deduplication while remaining excluded from active views and charts.

Research signals

Directness, time horizon, and contrary evidence.

Directness (0 to 3)

  • Target-like (3): studied sustained collaborative work close to real teams and repositories.
  • Adjacent (2): studied a nearby task, benchmark, or controlled workflow that transfers in part.
  • Analogy (1): studied a comparable domain such as classical automation or clinical teaming.
  • Conceptual (0): a theoretical framework or synthesis rather than a direct empirical trial.

Time horizon and negative findings

Horizons distinguish not observed, technical evaluation, observed once, observed repeatedly, and observed over a long period.

Rigorous negative, conditional, or null studies (for example, showing when human-AI combinations underperform the best single baseline or slow experienced developers) receive full evidentiary weight. StaleToast rewards decision-relevant evidence, never positive AI hype.

Repository signals

Eleven collaboration mechanisms against a pinned commit.

What is checked

README-screened repositories record eleven dimensions at a specific Git commit: collaboration topology, workflow state, human authority, memory context, trace recovery, evaluation surface, collaboration baseline, cost burden, change expiry, independent verification, and interoperability.

Five status codes, separate from stars

Each mechanism is marked Visible, Partial, Not observed, Out of scope, or Unknown. A README screen is not a code or security audit. Observed GitHub stars are dated popularity only and never influence editorial priority.

Weekly Hot 100 rubric

Seven-dimension collaborative intelligence scoring.

Starting with the November 23, 2026 scoring framework, weekly Hot 100 preparation evaluates pinned sources across seven explicit dimensions. For chart ordering only, completed pinned cards sort by weighted established points across those dimensions (breaking ties on strength of evidence, outcomes, and distinct contribution), while profiles keep all seven dimensions separate and never display a single composite score. Cards with any unassessed dimension or stale commit pin remain provisional until completed.

Dimensions and weights

  • Coordination and orchestration (20): handoffs, shared state, and conflict resolution that change downstream actions.
  • Human goals and direction (15): expressing constraints, defining success, and redirecting work.
  • Delegated authority and consent (15): clear boundaries for who approves, commits, intervenes, or revokes.
  • Idea into action (15): tracing decisions into real workflow or verified execution.
  • Outcomes and learning (15): quality, effort, and failure comparisons against a credible baseline.
  • Strength of evidence (15): rigor of study design, holdouts, pinned code, or independent checks.
  • Distinct contribution (5): added value and an observable local applicability check.

Anchor scale (0 to 4) and scope map

  • 0: checked absence or out of scope, with recorded reason.
  • 1: described intention or static mechanism claim.
  • 2: worked example or bounded prototype trace.
  • 3: demonstrated contribution with explicit transfer limits.
  • 4: matched comparison against a strong alternative or failure case.

Alongside the 17 bounded claims, the Scope Map tracks 11 collaborative-intelligence components, clearly separating the 7 established baseline components from 4 newly added components awaiting primary appraisal.

What StaleToast never claims. No profile or chart position certifies software safety, legal license clearance, replication, or fitness for production adoption. Always inspect the primary paper or pinned repository revision before relying on it.
